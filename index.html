About Us

The Presidential Graduate School (PGS) is an affiliated institution of Westcliff University. It offers various academic programs of Westcliff University here in Nepal.


Westcliff University

Westcliff University was established to provide quality education in the fields of Business,
Technology, and Education.It received its approval from the Bureau for Private Postsecondary
Education in 1993. It is accredited by the WASC Senior College and University Commission.
Westcliff’s programs are designed to prepare students for both theory and practical skills
needed in real jobs.The university has four colleges: Business, Education, Technology & Engineering,
and Law.The faculty have over 20 years of work experience and help students succeed in their careers.
