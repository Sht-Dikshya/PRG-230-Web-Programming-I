About Us

Eat smart. Live light.

Who We Are

NutriTime is more than just a food tracking app — it's a lifestyle shift for people who want to build better eating habits without the stress of complicated diets or daily workouts.

Our Vision

To create a world where smart eating is simple, sustainable, and accessible to all.

Our Mission

  • Develop food discipline through mindful tracking
  • Make nutrition simple and data-backed
  • Help users form better eating habits without fitness routines
  • Offer practical, personalized health insights

Why NutriTime?

Because most fitness apps ignore what matters most — when and what you eat.

  • Skipping meals due to work
  • Eating at random hours
  • Overeating out of stress
  • Not drinking enough water
  • Gaining weight despite low activity

Key Highlights

  • No workout required — just food habit support
  • Simple UI, perfect for all age groups
  • Based on behavioral science and nutrition principles
  • Helps detect food triggers and timing issues
  • Supports your journey whether for weight loss, digestion, or energy

Our Promise

  • Keep your data private and secure
  • Deliver an ad-free, focused experience
  • Continuously improve based on user feedback
  • Support your health journey at every step

Join the Movement

Be part of the mindful eating revolution. Thousands of users are already making small, daily changes to reclaim their health — one meal at a time.

Because your body deserves better — and it starts with when and what you eat.
